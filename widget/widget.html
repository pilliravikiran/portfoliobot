RaviBot widget demo

This is a standalone test page. Look in the bottom-right corner — the blue/purple chat bubble is the widget. Click it to chat.

To embed the widget in Portfolio/index.html, copy the single <script> tag from widget.js and drop it just before the closing </body> tag of your page. Make sure api.py is running: uvicorn api:app --port 8000.

You can change the colours, position, and API endpoint at the top of widget.js.
